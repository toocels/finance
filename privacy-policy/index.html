Policies & Help

Privacy Policy

How Jeeves Investment Advisors collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the contact form on this website, we collect the information you choose to submit: full name, email address, phone number (optional), topic of enquiry, and your message. We do not collect any financial account, banking, or KYC information through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted via the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry. It is never sold, rented, or used for unsolicited marketing.

3. Where It Is Stored & Who Processes It

Contact form submissions are relayed via Web3Forms directly to the Principal Officer's email inbox. Submissions are not stored in the public Google Sheet used elsewhere on this site — that sheet contains only aggregate, non-identifying SEBI complaint-count data, never contact-form or client information.

web3forms.com ↗ (opens in new tab)

See our Complaints Data page

4. Retention

Submitted information is retained in the Principal Officer's email inbox for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and resolve your enquiry, or until you request its deletion.

5. Correction & Deletion Requests

To request correction or deletion of information you have submitted, contact the Principal & Compliance Officer, Madhu Srinivas V, at vmadhusrinivas@gmail.com.

vmadhusrinivas@gmail.com

6. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Web3Forms — relays contact form submissions to our email.
  • Google Sheets — hosts only aggregate, public SEBI complaint-count data; no personal or contact-form data is ever placed here.
  • Dynadot — domain registrar and DNS for this website.
  • GitHub Pages — static hosting for this website.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and does not use Cloudflare or any other proxy/CDN service that sets security cookies. This section will be updated if analytics or a CDN are added in the future.

8. Consent

By submitting the contact form on this website, you consent to your information being processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
