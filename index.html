Investor Charter for Investment Advisers (Annexure F)

As prescribed by the SEBI Master Circular for Investment Advisers ↗ (opens in new tab), investors are entitled to:

A. Rights of Investors

  • Receive unbiased and tailored investment advice based on personal risk profile.
  • Receive all disclosures regarding fees, conflicts of interest, and regulatory details.
  • Transparent fee payment exclusively through banking channels with receipts.
  • Prompt grievance redressal via adviser and official regulatory portals (SCORES / SMART ODR).

B. Responsibilities of Investors

  • Provide accurate, complete financial and risk profiling information.
  • Sign formal client agreement and adhere to mutually agreed terms.
  • Never share banking/trading credentials or permit discretionary trading.
  • Verify SEBI registration credentials before engaging an investment adviser.
