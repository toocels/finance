Methodology

Our Approach

A structured, 7-step collaborative journey from discovery to lifelong wealth management.

  1. 1

    Discovery

    Understanding your life goals, family situation, cash flows, and existing investments to build a complete financial picture.

  2. 2

    Onboarding

    Formalising the client agreement with full fee disclosure, as required under SEBI Investment Adviser Regulations.

  3. 3

    Documentation

    Collecting KYC, risk-profiling inputs, and records of your existing portfolio and financial statements.

  4. 4

    Planning

    Translating your goals and risk profile into a structured, actionable financial plan.

  5. 5

    Plan Approval

    Reviewing the proposed plan together, addressing questions, and securing your sign-off.

  6. 6

    Implementation

    Providing assistance for executing the investment plan as and when required.

  7. 7

    Ongoing Tracking

    Periodic review and rebalancing to keep your portfolio aligned with your goals over time.
